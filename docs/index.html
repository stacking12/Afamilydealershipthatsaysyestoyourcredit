Our Story

About Our NJ Buy Here Pay Here

NJ Buy Here Pay Here started with a simple belief: getting behind the wheel of a reliable car shouldn't depend on a perfect credit score. As a family dealership based on Broad St in Newark, we built our entire financing process in-house so we could make that decision ourselves, instead of leaving it up to a bank.

That's what makes us a true Buy Here Pay Here dealership — we sell the car and finance it, all under one roof. It means faster approvals, more flexibility on payments, and a team that actually wants to see you drive away happy, not just get you to sign paperwork.

Today, we're proud to serve families across Newark and the surrounding NJ community — including many Spanish-speaking families — with quality used cars, honest guidance, and financing built around real life.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Buy Here Pay Here and how is it different from a bank loan? +
Buy Here Pay Here means we finance the car ourselves, right here at the dealership. There's no bank loan application, no third-party lender, and no long wait for approval — you buy the car and make your payments directly to us.
Can I get approved with bad credit or no credit? +
Yes. We accept all credit situations, including bad credit, no credit, past repossessions, or bankruptcy. Approval is based on your ability to make payments today, not your credit history.
How fast can I drive away in a car? +
Most customers are approved the same day they visit. Bring your ID, proof of income, and proof of residence, and in many cases you can drive off the lot that same day.
Do you require a large down payment? +
We work with flexible payment plans designed around what you can afford. Down payment amounts vary by vehicle, and our team will walk you through options that fit your budget.
Do you have Spanish-speaking staff? +
Yes, our team includes Spanish-speaking staff so we can walk you through financing and paperwork clearly in the language you're most comfortable with.
